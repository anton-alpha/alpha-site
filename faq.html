Your money

How is Alpha different from my bank?

Alpha does everything you use a bank for: hold your money, pay, get paid, earn. The difference is where your money lives. At a bank it is a deposit on their balance sheet, which they lend out and can freeze. On Alpha it is held as fully reserved digital dollars, redeemable one-to-one, in an account only you control. Nobody, including Alpha, can read it, move it or lend it.

Where is my money, exactly?

In your own account on the ledger, held as regulated, fully reserved digital dollars backed one-to-one by US dollars and short-dated US government debt. Alpha never holds it. The ledger proves every second that it holds every dollar it owes, and anyone can check.

Can Alpha freeze, move or lend my money?

No. Your Spend balance sits under keys only you hold, and Alpha has no master key. Alpha can pause the services it runs, the card, local money or Earn, in an emergency, and never your Spend balance.

Can I get my money out whenever I want?

Yes. Move it anywhere, any time, with your passkey. You never need Alpha's permission.

Why dollars?

Because the dollar is the currency the world settles in. Your balance stays in dollars wherever you live, so inflation or a falling currency at home doesn't touch it, and it converts to local money only at the moment you spend or send, at a rate you see first.

Does my money earn anything?

Yes, if you opt in. Earn pays a floating rate on fully reserved dollars, and your money is back in Spend within 48 hours whenever you ask. Platinum Global and Sovereign Global carry higher Earn rates and limits.

Privacy and security

Can Alpha see my payments?

No. Your balance and your payments are sealed on your phone before they leave it, and unlock only on your own devices. You hold the only key to the sealed record. Not a partner, not a bank, not Alpha can read it.

What if I lose my phone?

Sign in on a new phone with Face ID and a recovery contact. Lose every device and any two of your three guardians, your backup key, a person you trust and Alpha, can let you back in after a 72-hour delay you can stop from any device if it wasn't you.

Is there a password?

No. A passkey on your own phone, approved with Face ID, authorises every payment. There is no password to leak, no reset link to phish and no code by text to intercept. Sending to someone new asks for Face ID a second time.

What happens if someone steals my card?

Freeze it in one tap. The card spends only from its own Card balance, topped up from Spend under a limit you set, so the rest of your money is never exposed.

Prices and plans

What does it cost?

The account and card are free. Payments between Alpha accounts are free. Local money in or out costs 0.25% at the real rate, or 0% on Platinum Global and Sovereign Global. There is no foreign card fee. Every $AG you earn is valued at US$1 against any Alpha fee.

What are the plans?

Elite Global is free: the card, free payments, Earn, pots, invoices and $AG rewards. Platinum Global is US$24.99 a month, or free if you hold US$1,000 of $AG: a metal card, 0% Alpha fee on local money, higher Earn rate and limits. Sovereign Global is US$199 a month, or free if you hold US$25,000 of $AG: a heavy numbered card engraved to you, 0% Alpha fee on everything, the highest Earn rate and limits, a named person at Alpha any hour, and first access to every new country.

What is $AG?

Alpha's reward. You earn it on card spending and for bringing friends. Every $AG is valued at US$1 across Alpha and pays your fees or your plan. It is earned, not sold, and it never expires.

Is there a business account?

Yes. Business is US$199 a month for up to five people with team cards, invoices, bulk payouts and roles. Scale is US$999 a month for up to 25 people with approvals above a threshold, accounting exports, API and priority support. Enterprise is custom, with key rotation, a custody policy and dedicated support.

Using Alpha

Which countries?

The card works everywhere the card network is accepted, which is every country on earth, and payments between Alpha accounts reach any country in under a second. Opening an account depends on your country of residence; join the waitlist and we'll tell you when yours is live.

How fast is a payment?

Between Alpha accounts, under a second, any hour of any day. To an account outside Alpha, as fast as the receiving system allows, with the exact amount shown before you approve.

How do I get paid?

Send an invoice, share your @handle, or give out your own account details. Clients pay by bank or card in their own currency and you receive dollars, same day. Payments from other Alpha accounts land instantly and free.

Can I pay bills and rent in my own currency?

Yes. Local money goes out to any local bank account at a rate you see before you tap, 0.25% on Elite Global and 0% on the paid plans. Your balance stays in dollars until the moment you send.
