Security

Nothing to steal. Nothing to phish.

Most account theft starts with a password or a reset link. Alpha has neither.

Your face, not a passwordA passkey on your own phone approves every payment. There is no password to leak and no code by text to intercept.
No master keyAlpha holds no key that opens your account. There is nothing inside Alpha for an attacker to take.
Lose every device, keep your moneyTwo of three guardians can let you back in: your backup key, a person you trust, and Alpha. After a 72-hour delay you can stop if it wasn't you.
Rules locked in codeThe rules that hold your money are published once and never altered. No update can quietly change what they do.
New recipient? Your face againSending to someone new asks for your face a second time. No waiting period, no way for a thief to add themselves.
Limits that follow youRolling 30-day limits you set yourself, raised only with your face, so a bad day can't become a catastrophe.
